Procedures & Treatments

Dr. Hamoui offers a full range of minimally invasive and advanced urologic procedures — from robotic surgery and laser stone treatment to hormone therapy and implantable devices.

BPH Treatment ⭐ Center of Excellence

UroLift

Minimally invasive BPH treatment using small permanent implants to relieve urinary obstruction — no cutting, heating, or tissue removal. Same-day discharge, preserved sexual function.

Learn more BPH Treatment ⭐ Center of Excellence

Aquablation

Robotically-guided, heat-free waterjet ablation of the prostate. Effective for prostates of any size with lower retrograde ejaculation rates than traditional TURP.

Learn more Hormone Therapy

BioTE Hormone Therapy

Bioidentical hormone pellet therapy for men and women. Consistent, steady-release testosterone and estrogen to address fatigue, low libido, menopause symptoms, and more.

Learn more Robotic Surgery

Da Vinci Robotic Surgery

Minimally invasive robotic surgery for prostate cancer, kidney tumors, and complex urologic conditions. Dr. Hamoui is trained by Intuitive Surgical and has trained other surgeons.

Learn more Kidney Stones

Ureteroscopy & Laser Lithotripsy

Gold-standard treatment for kidney and ureteral stones. A flexible scope passes through the urinary tract to reach the stone — no incisions — and a laser pulverizes it.

Learn more Bladder Control

SNS Nerve Stimulators

Sacral neuromodulation with Medtronic InterStim or Axonics for overactive bladder, urge incontinence, and urinary retention that hasn't responded to medications.

Learn more Erectile Dysfunction ⭐ Center of Excellence

Boston Scientific IPP

Inflatable penile prosthesis for men with ED who have not responded to other treatments. Highest patient satisfaction of any ED therapy — permanent, on-demand, and fully concealed.
