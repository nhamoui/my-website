Boston Scientific Inflatable Penile Prosthesis

An inflatable penile prosthesis (IPP) is a surgically implanted device that gives men with erectile dysfunction complete, on-demand control over erections. For men who have failed or cannot tolerate oral medications, injections, or other ED therapies, the IPP offers a permanent, highly satisfying solution with the highest patient and partner satisfaction rates of any ED treatment.

Boston Scientific Center of Excellence

Dr. Hamoui's practice is a designated Boston Scientific Center of Excellence for penile prosthesis implantation, recognizing his surgical volume, clinical outcomes, and expertise in inflatable penile prosthesis surgery.

How It Works

  • Two inflatable cylinders implanted in the erectile chambers
  • Fluid reservoir behind the pubic bone; small pump in the scrotum
  • Squeeze pump = erection; press release valve = deflation
  • Entire system is concealed and invisible when flaccid
  • Outpatient procedure, ~1 hour under general or spinal anesthesia

Who Is a Candidate

  • ED not controlled by oral medications (PDE5 inhibitors)
  • Post-prostatectomy ED after prostate cancer surgery
  • ED from Peyronie's disease, diabetes, or vascular disease
  • Failed penile injections or vacuum erection devices
  • Prefer a permanent, medication-free solution

What to Expect

  • Mild scrotal swelling for 2–4 weeks post-op
  • Device activation and training at 4–6 weeks
  • Return to sexual activity by ~6 weeks
  • Patient satisfaction rates exceed 90% — highest of any ED treatment
  • 15+ year device longevity; Boston Scientific lifetime warranty on components
