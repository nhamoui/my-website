Conditions We Treat

Dr. Hamoui provides expert diagnosis and treatment for the full spectrum of urological conditions across all four categories below.

Home›Conditions

Oncology

Specialized care for urologic cancers using the latest surgical, minimally invasive, and collaborative oncology approaches.

Prostate Cancer

The most common cancer in men. Dr. Hamoui offers active surveillance, robotic prostatectomy, and coordination with radiation oncology.

Learn more

Bladder Cancer

From TURBT and intravesical BCG therapy to radical cystectomy — comprehensive bladder cancer management and surveillance.

Learn more

Kidney Cancer

Minimally invasive partial and radical nephrectomy for renal tumors, with active surveillance protocols for small incidental masses.

Learn more

Testicular Cancer

Highly curable when caught early. Expert orchiectomy and coordination with medical oncology for staging and further treatment.

Learn more

Men's Health

Confidential, comprehensive evaluation and treatment for conditions affecting men's quality of life and sexual health.

Erectile Dysfunction

From lifestyle modification and oral medications to penile prosthesis — full range of ED treatment options.

Learn more

Enlarged Prostate (BPH)

Medical and minimally invasive surgical options including UroLift® and Rezūm™ water vapor therapy for BPH.

Learn more

Prostatitis

Targeted treatment for acute bacterial, chronic bacterial, and chronic pelvic pain syndrome presentations.

Learn more

Low Testosterone

Lab-confirmed hypogonadism managed with individualized testosterone replacement therapy and lifestyle support.

Learn more

Peyronie's Disease

Non-surgical and surgical correction of penile curvature caused by fibrous scar tissue, including Xiaflex injections.

Learn more

Kidney & Bladder

Comprehensive management of kidney stone disease, bladder dysfunction, incontinence, and urinary tract infections.

Kidney Stones

Complete stone management from medical expulsive therapy to ureteroscopy with laser lithotripsy and PCNL.

Learn more

Overactive Bladder

Behavioral therapy, medications, Botox bladder injections, and neuromodulation for urinary urgency and frequency.

Learn more

Urinary Incontinence

Conservative and surgical management of stress, urge, and mixed urinary incontinence in men and women.

Learn more

Urinary Tract Infections

Evaluation and treatment of UTIs, with focused workup for recurrent infections to identify underlying causes.

Learn more

Women's Health

Specialized urogynecologic care for bladder, pelvic floor, and voiding disorders affecting women.

Female Urinary Incontinence

Pelvic floor therapy, pessary fitting, midurethral sling, and neuromodulation for all types of female incontinence.

Learn more

Pelvic Organ Prolapse

Conservative and surgical repair for cystocele, uterine prolapse, and vault prolapse including robotic sacrocolpopexy.

Learn more

Interstitial Cystitis

Multimodal management of chronic bladder pain syndrome including dietary modification, instillations, and neuromodulation.

Learn more
