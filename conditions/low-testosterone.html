Men's Health

Low Testosterone

Low testosterone (hypogonadism) occurs when the testes produce insufficient testosterone. It affects energy, mood, body composition, and sexual function — and is reliably diagnosed with blood testing and a thorough clinical evaluation.

Symptoms

  • Persistent fatigue and low energy
  • Decreased libido and sexual desire
  • Erectile dysfunction
  • Loss of muscle mass or increased body fat
  • Depressed mood, irritability, or brain fog

Causes & Risk Factors

  • Age-related decline (natural after 30)
  • Obesity or type 2 diabetes
  • Pituitary or hypothalamic disorders
  • Prior chemotherapy, radiation, or steroid use

Treatment Options We Offer

  • Testosterone replacement therapy (injections, gels, or patches)
  • Clomiphene citrate (fertility-preserving option)
  • Lifestyle optimization (weight, sleep, exercise)
  • Evaluation and treatment of secondary causes
  • Ongoing lab monitoring and dose adjustment
