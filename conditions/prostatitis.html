Men's Health

Prostatitis

Prostatitis is inflammation of the prostate gland and the most common urologic diagnosis in men under 50. It ranges from acute bacterial infection to chronic pelvic pain syndrome — accurate diagnosis is key to effective treatment.

Symptoms

  • Pelvic, perineal, or rectal pain
  • Painful or burning urination
  • Urinary urgency and frequency
  • Pain during or after ejaculation
  • Fever and chills (acute bacterial form)

Causes & Risk Factors

  • Bacterial infection (typically E. coli)
  • Prior urinary tract infection or catheterization
  • Pelvic floor muscle dysfunction
  • High psychological stress

Treatment Options We Offer

  • Antibiotic therapy (bacterial prostatitis)
  • Alpha-blockers for urinary symptoms
  • NSAIDs for pain management
  • Pelvic floor physical therapy
  • Multimodal care for chronic CPPS
