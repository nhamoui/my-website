Meet Dr. Hamoui

A Native Floridian Dedicated to Excellence

Urologist & Interventional Radiologist — Brooksville & Hudson, FL

Dr. Nabeel Hamoui is a native Floridian, born in Brooksville, FL. He received his medical degree from Duke University School of Medicine in Durham, North Carolina. He completed his residencies in urology and diagnostic radiology and fellowship training in interventional radiology at Northwestern Memorial Hospital in Chicago, IL.

He has served as an instructor for Intuitive Surgical and has trained other surgeons in robotic surgery. He is a recognized leader in surgical stone treatment, prostate surgery, robotic surgery, and ED treatments.

He is a member of the American Urological Association, Society of Interventional Radiology, and the Radiology Society of North America.
