Biography

A Native Floridian Dedicated to Excellence

Dr. Nabeel Hamoui is a native Floridian, born in Brooksville, FL. He received his medical degree from Duke University School of Medicine in Durham, North Carolina, where he also served as a Fellow for the National Institutes of Health. Dr. Hamoui also holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree from the Kellogg School of Management at Northwestern University.

Dr. Hamoui completed his training in radiology with a fellowship in interventional radiology as well as his urology training at Northwestern Memorial Hospital, Chicago. He also served as Chief Resident in Urology from July 2017 to June 2018. He is board certified in diagnostic and interventional radiology as well as urology. He has served as an instructor for Intuitive Surgical and has trained other surgeons in robotic surgery.

He is a member of the American Urological Association, Society of Interventional Radiology, and the Radiology Society of North America.

Philosophy of Care

As a native of Brooksville, Dr. Hamoui is deeply committed to the health of the communities he grew up in. His unique dual board certification in urology and interventional radiology allows him to offer patients a broader range of minimally invasive options than most urologists — combining the precision of image-guided techniques with the full spectrum of urologic surgery.

He believes that well-informed patients are empowered patients, and dedicates time to ensuring every individual thoroughly understands their diagnosis and all available treatment paths.

Surgical Expertise & Teaching

Dr. Hamoui has served as an instructor for Intuitive Surgical, the maker of the da Vinci robotic surgical system, training fellow surgeons in robotic-assisted techniques. This depth of experience translates into safer, more efficient surgeries and better outcomes for his patients.
