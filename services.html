Conditions & Treatments

Our Areas of Expertise

We provide comprehensive diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up care using the latest minimally invasive and evidence-based approaches.

Kidney Stone Management

Kidney stones affect millions of Americans and can cause severe pain and urinary obstruction. Dr. Hamoui provides comprehensive stone evaluation, prevention strategies, and state-of-the-art surgical removal when needed.

Ureteroscopy with Laser Lithotripsy Shock Wave Lithotripsy (SWL) Percutaneous Nephrolithotomy (PCNL) 24-Hour Urine Metabolic Testing Stone Prevention Counseling

Prostate Health

The prostate is a source of several common and serious conditions in men. Dr. Hamoui offers thorough evaluation and a full range of treatments, from watchful waiting to advanced surgical therapy.

PSA Screening & Risk Assessment Prostate Biopsy (Fusion-Guided) Active Surveillance Protocols Robotic Prostatectomy (RALP) BPH & LUTS Treatment (TURP, UroLift)

Bladder Conditions

Bladder disorders range from common and highly treatable to complex and chronic. Our practice manages the full spectrum, including cancer surveillance for high-risk patients.

Overactive Bladder (OAB) Urinary Incontinence Bladder Cancer (TURBT, Surveillance) Interstitial Cystitis Recurrent UTI Evaluation

Urologic Oncology

Urologic cancers require specialized, multidisciplinary care. Dr. Hamoui has fellowship-level training in urologic cancer surgery and coordinates with medical oncology and radiation oncology to deliver comprehensive cancer care.

Prostate Cancer Kidney (Renal) Cancer Bladder Cancer Testicular Cancer Adrenal Tumors

Minimally Invasive & Robotic Surgery

Dr. Hamoui is fellowship-trained in robotic-assisted laparoscopic surgery, offering patients smaller incisions, less blood loss, reduced hospital stays, and faster return to normal activities compared to open surgery.

Robotic Prostatectomy Robotic Partial Nephrectomy Robotic Radical Nephrectomy Laparoscopic Pyeloplasty Laparoscopic Adrenalectomy

Male Sexual Health & Infertility

Issues with sexual function and fertility can significantly impact quality of life. Dr. Hamoui provides confidential, judgment-free evaluation and effective treatment options for men of all ages.

Erectile Dysfunction Male Infertility & Semen Analysis Testosterone Deficiency Peyronie's Disease Vasectomy & Vasectomy Reversal

Kidney & Adrenal Disorders

Beyond kidney stones and cancer, the kidneys and adrenal glands can be affected by cysts, ureteropelvic junction (UPJ) obstruction, and hormonal tumors. Dr. Hamoui evaluates and manages these conditions with precision.

Complex Renal Cysts UPJ Obstruction (Pyeloplasty) Adrenal Mass Evaluation Adrenalectomy Hematuria Workup

General Urology & Preventive Care

Routine urological health maintenance is important at every age. We provide annual wellness visits, cancer screenings, and management of common conditions like UTIs, epididymitis, and hydrocele.

Annual Urologic Wellness Exam Urinary Tract Infections (UTI) Epididymitis & Orchitis Hydrocele & Varicocele Hematuria Evaluation
